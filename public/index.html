FLASHING GUIDE

How to Flash iPhone with 3uTools

Step 1

Download & Install 3uTools

  1. Visit the official 3uTools website: https://www.3u.com
  2. Click on "Download" to get the latest version
  3. Select your operating system:
    • Windows: Download the Windows installer (.exe)
    • macOS: Download the macOS version
  4. Run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts
  5. Complete the installation by clicking "Finish"
⚠️ Note: 3uTools is primarily for Windows. macOS users can use Apple Configurator 2 or other flashing tools.
Step 2

Prepare Your iPhone

  1. Back up your data using iCloud or iTunes (flashing will erase your device)
  2. Disable Find My iPhone:
    • Open Settings → [Your Name] → Find My
    • Tap Find My iPhone → Toggle OFF
    • Enter your Apple ID password if prompted
  3. Note your iPhone's UDID (Unique Device Identifier) - you'll need this in 3uTools
  4. Have a USB cable ready (preferably original or certified)
Step 3

Download iPhone Firmware (IPSW)

  1. Go back to the Firmware Finder section above
  2. Select iPhone from the product buttons
  3. Choose your specific iPhone model from the device dropdown
  4. Select your desired iOS version from the version dropdown
  5. Click the ⬇️ Download Firmware button to download the .ipsw file
  6. Save the file to a known location on your computer (e.g., Desktop or Downloads)
💾 Tip: IPSW files are typically 4-5 GB. Ensure you have enough storage space.
Step 4

Connect iPhone to 3uTools

  1. Launch 3uTools on your computer
  2. Connect your iPhone to your computer using a USB cable
  3. On your iPhone, tap "Trust" when prompted to trust this computer
  4. Enter your iPhone's passcode if prompted
  5. 3uTools should automatically detect your device and display:
    • Device name and model
    • Current iOS version
    • Serial number and UDID
⚠️ Important: If your device isn't recognized, try:
  • Reinstall iTunes or Apple Mobile Device Support
  • Try a different USB port or cable
  • Restart both your iPhone and computer
Step 5

Flash iPhone with New Firmware

  1. In 3uTools, look for the "Flash" or "Restore" button (usually in the main interface or under "Tools")
  2. Click the button and select "Browse" or "Select File"
  3. Navigate to the .ipsw firmware file you downloaded in Step 3
  4. Select the file and click "Open" or "Confirm"
  5. Review the options:
    • Preserve data (optional): Check if you want to keep data (not always reliable)
    • Backup (recommended): Create a backup before flashing
  6. Click "Flash" or "Start" to begin the process
Step 6

Monitor Flashing Progress

  1. The flashing process will begin and display a progress bar
  2. Your iPhone will:
    • Restart into Recovery Mode (Apple logo may appear)
    • Show various status messages
    • Progress through extraction and flashing steps
  3. DO NOT disconnect your iPhone or force quit 3uTools during this time
  4. The process typically takes 5-15 minutes depending on file size and system speed
⏱️ Timing: The progress bar may sometimes pause momentarily - this is normal.
Step 7

Complete the Setup

  1. When flashing completes, 3uTools will show a "Success" message
  2. Your iPhone will automatically restart with the new iOS version
  3. On your iPhone:
    • You'll see the "Hello" setup screen
    • Select your language and follow the setup wizard
    • Enter your Apple ID credentials
    • Optionally restore from your backup (if you created one)
  4. Safely disconnect your iPhone from the USB cable
  5. Verify the iOS version in Settings → General → About

Troubleshooting Common Issues

❌ "Device not recognized"

Install or update iTunes, USB drivers, and Apple Mobile Device Service. Try different USB ports and cables.

❌ "Error 3, 11, or 13"

These are USB connection errors. Try a different USB cable or computer port. Disable antivirus software temporarily.

❌ "iPhone stuck in Recovery Mode"

Try flashing again with the correct .ipsw file. Force restart the device (power + volume buttons) and reconnect.

❌ "Firmware mismatch error"

Ensure you're using the .ipsw file for your exact iPhone model. Check the filename contains your model identifier.

❌ "Find My iPhone lock"

If you forgot your Apple ID, you'll see an Activation Lock. Sign in with your Apple ID or visit iforgot.apple.com

Safety Tips & Best Practices

  • ✅ Always back up your data before flashing
  • ✅ Use a reliable USB connection - avoid USB hubs if possible
  • ✅ Keep your computer plugged in during the process
  • ✅ Close unnecessary programs to avoid interruptions
  • ✅ Disable Find My iPhone before flashing
  • ✅ Download from trusted sources only (like FallyTech)
  • ❌ Never disconnect your device during flashing
  • ❌ Never update firmware while on low battery
  • ❌ Don't use your device while flashing is in progress
